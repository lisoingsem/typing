# Khmer practice corpus

The word practice list is a small, hand-reviewed corpus for typing practice. Common-word selection and ordering were checked against:

- **Khmer Chuon Nath Dictionary data**, digitized by the Open Institute through the USAID-funded SPICE program and distributed under LGPL-2.1: <https://github.com/interscript/khmer-dict-spice>
- **Khmer LineBreaking Dictionary**, a frequency-based Khmer word list from SIL NRSI, distributed under the MIT License: <https://github.com/silnrsi/khmerlbdict>

Modern everyday terms and the practice sentences were manually reviewed for this project. Corpus strings are stored in the same normalized Khmer Unicode order used by the app. The beginner list intentionally contains complete words only; it does not contain unfinished spelling fragments.
